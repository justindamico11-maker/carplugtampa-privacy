Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Who we are

The Car Plug Tampa ("we," "us," "our") is an auto broker based in Tampa, Florida. We shop multiple dealerships for you and help connect you with financing options. We are not a lender and do not make credit decisions. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or fill out one of our forms (including Facebook and Instagram lead forms), and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill out our form or contact us, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City and/or ZIP code
  • Your interest in a down payment and/or trade-in
  • Best time to contact you
  • Your consent to be contacted by call or text
  • Anything else you choose to tell us when we talk

If you submit a form through Facebook or Instagram, Meta also shares basic form details with us. Meta's own use of your data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about the vehicle you're looking for
  • To search dealership inventory that fits your needs and budget
  • To help connect you with financing options
  • To answer your questions and follow up on your request
  • To keep records and meet legal requirements

Sharing with partner dealerships and lenders

To help you find a vehicle and financing options, we may share your information with our partner dealerships and lenders or finance providers they work with. They use it to check inventory, contact you about vehicles, and review financing options. Those businesses have their own privacy policies.

We may also share information with service providers who help us run our business (for example, texting or email tools), only as needed to provide those services, and when required by law.

We do not sell your personal data

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

Calls and text messages (SMS)

By submitting our form and checking the consent box, you agree that The Car Plug Tampa and our partner dealerships may contact you at the phone number you provided by call or text, including with automated technology, about your vehicle and financing request. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

To stop texts, reply STOP at any time. Reply HELP for help. You can also ask us to stop calling you, or email us at the address below. We will honor your request promptly.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as needed to help with your vehicle search and follow up, generally no more than 24 months after our last contact with you, unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Security

We use reasonable steps to protect your information, such as limiting access to people who need it and using secure, password-protected accounts. No method of storing or sending data is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email us.

Children under 18

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.
